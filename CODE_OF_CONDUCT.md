# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We are committed to making participation in Open Project Hub a welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free experience for everyone.

We welcome contributions from people regardless of background or experience level.

## Expected Behavior

Examples of positive behavior include:

* Being respectful and welcoming.
* Giving constructive feedback.
* Accepting different opinions.
* Focusing on what is best for the community.
* Helping new contributors learn.

## Unacceptable Behavior

The following behavior is not tolerated:

* Harassment or discrimination.
* Personal attacks.
* Trolling or deliberately disruptive behavior.
* Publishing private information without permission.
* Threats or intimidation.
* Any behavior that makes the community unsafe or unwelcoming.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may:

* Warn contributors.
* Remove inappropriate content.
* Temporarily restrict participation.
* Permanently ban participation in serious or repeated cases.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, contact the repository maintainers through GitHub.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to all project spaces and community interactions associated with Open Project Hub.
